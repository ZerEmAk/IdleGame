# Incremental Game Research

Comparative study completed August 13, 2026. These are design observations and
project-specific lessons, not quotations. Official game pages, developer pages,
repositories, or maintained references are linked for follow-up.

## Repeated findings

1. **Progressive disclosure beats a disabled catalogue.** Reveal a resource or
   panel when its first meaningful source/use appears.
2. **Manual understanding should become earned automation.** Researching away a
   learned chore is more satisfying than an arbitrary speed bonus.
3. **Side systems must feed the main economy.** Minigame rewards, unlocks, or
   automation should matter outside the minigame.
4. **A resource needs a role, not merely a color.** Give it a distinct pace,
   multiple eventual sources, and multiple sinks.
5. **Active play needs a ceiling.** Skill can improve efficiency and rare finds,
   but should not invalidate idle progress.
6. **Exact information is QoL.** Show caps, net rates, continuous drains, ETAs,
   requirements, disabled reasons, and before/after effects.
7. **Personality is efficient production value.** Names, flavor, event prose,
   animation, and visible workers create attachment without expensive art.
8. **QoL can be progression.** Queues, filters, bulk actions, presets, and
   automation are compelling rewards because they retire mastered friction.
9. **Prestige must change decisions.** If added later, it should introduce a new
   tree, constraint, phase, or automation layer—not just `×2`.

## Applied in v0.3

- Hidden late resources and jobs use shared visibility requirements.
- Focused Excavation and Séance give bounded rewards tied to the core economy.
- Research is timed, exact, continuously funded, pausable, and queued.
- Skeletons have stable IDs, editable names, multiple data-driven jobs, and
  visible work/lifetime status.
- Events are structured and filterable; tooltips combine mechanics and flavor.
- Dragging uses a visible proxy, valid/invalid targets, snap/reject feedback,
  and the existing select-then-slot alternative.

## Best next features

1. A finite Apprentice chapter ending and a crypt/coven phase transition.
2. A memorial ledger with lifetime work statistics for expired Skeletons.
3. Short Skeleton expeditions using worker + route + risk + timer + reward.
4. Research-earned automation for mastered minigames and workshop recipes.
5. Exact time-to-afford and source/use lines in tooltips.
6. Bulk upgrade controls only after repeatable producers exist.
7. Save export/import and a detailed offline summary.
8. Later specializations, equipment, and saved worker assignment presets.
