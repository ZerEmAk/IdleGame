Changes to The First Rite.

v0.4.0 — The Veiled Ledger August 13, 2026
  • Overhauled every room with distinct visual accents and replaceable placeholder art.
  • Rebuilt Overview as a live command desk with objectives, production, and operation status.
  • Turned Research into a compact, fogged discipline tree and added a completed Upgrade archive.
  • Connected the seven assembly hexes, reduced the Soul Gem, and persistently shuffled loose parts.
  • Fixed the drag proxy, reduced hidden-panel work, and removed duplicate sidebar controls.
v0.3.1 — The Rune Circle August 13, 2026
  • Rebuilt Skeleton Assembly as a seven-hex ritual lattice.
  • Fixed dragged parts becoming pinned to the viewport corner.
  • Expanded Séance to a 4 × 4 rune circle and six-rune memory sequence.
  • Focused Excavation now freezes and judges the exact visible strike position.
  • Added stronger success, failure, reveal, placement, and timing feedback.
v0.3.0 — The Apprentice's Workshop August 13, 2026
  • Added timed, continuously funded research with a planning queue.
  • Added Grave Dust, Ectoplasm, Ritual Ink, Relics, and workshop recipes.
  • Added Focused Excavation and Séance minigames.
  • Added editable skeleton names and three unlockable jobs.
  • Added categorized events, definition-driven tooltips, animations, and richer flavor text.
  • Split content and simulation into reusable framework systems with extension documentation.
v0.2.0 — Studies & Servants August 13, 2026
  • Added eight resource upgrades and eleven research projects.
  • Added multiple independently assigned skeletons.
  • Expanded assembly into a seven-part drag-and-drop minigame.
  • Added Overview, Rituals, Assembly, Skeletons, Upgrades, Research, and Stats tabs.
  • Slowed the opening progression and strengthened upgrade effects.
  • Standardized Settings, save controls, resources, and the Event Log.
v0.1.0 — The First Raising August 13, 2026
  • Introduced Mana channeling, grave digging, the Soul Cage, assembly, and temporary servants.
